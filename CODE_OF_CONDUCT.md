# Code of Conduct

2048 Nova is intended to be a welcoming, respectful, technically constructive open-source project. This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project-managed community spaces.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully, even when disagreeing;
- focus criticism on code, behavior, evidence, and design rather than people;
- provide reproducible technical details when possible;
- be patient with contributors of different experience levels;
- respect privacy and do not expose another person's private information;
- accept reasonable maintainer decisions about scope, safety, licensing, and release quality;
- correct mistakes when they are identified;
- avoid intentionally misleading claims about test/build/release status.

## Unacceptable behavior

The project does not accept:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory abuse;
- sexualized or hostile conduct directed at participants;
- doxxing or publishing private contact/credential information;
- impersonation intended to deceive contributors or users;
- spam, scams, malicious links, or credential requests;
- deliberately destructive contributions or attempts to introduce malware/secrets;
- repeated personal attacks in code review;
- knowingly fabricated bug reports, verification evidence, or release claims.

## Technical disagreement

Strong technical disagreement is allowed. Keep discussion grounded in the implementation, tests, documentation, platform behavior, or reproducible measurements.

Maintainers may ask for a minimal reproduction, regression test, source reference, benchmark method, or narrower change before accepting a claim or contribution.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, close, or remove project content that violates these expectations. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent participation restrictions in project-managed spaces.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and should avoid publishing unnecessary private details.

## Reporting community concerns

For moderation concerns that should not be handled publicly, contact:

**supportramsandesh@gmail.com**

Do not include passwords, tokens, private keys, payment credentials, or unrelated private information in a report.

Security vulnerabilities should follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), especially when public disclosure could increase risk.

## Scope

This code of conduct governs participation in spaces controlled by the 2048 Nova project. It does not claim authority over unrelated personal activity outside those spaces.
